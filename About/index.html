Who We Are

About E-Vidya Academy

Empowering the people of Odisha through accessible, high-quality online education.

🎓 Welcome to E-Vidya Academy

E-Vidya Academy is a platform dedicated to empowering the people of Odisha through high-quality educational assistance services. Our mission is to provide accessible and effective online learning tools — including tuition support for parents to enhance the academic growth of their children, as well as training in English communication, legal awareness, and mind power development.

We believe that quality education should not be limited by geography or resources. That is why E-Vidya Academy is specifically designed to serve rural and semi-urban communities across Odisha.

🛠️ Our Services

At E-Vidya Academy, we offer services designed to meet the educational and practical needs of our communities:

Special Training for Villagers
Online training sessions every Monday, Wednesday, and Friday focusing on:
  • English language skills
  • Legal literacy (for awareness purposes only)
  • Mind power and personality development
These sessions include pre-recorded videos, notes, and learning materials intended for personal use only.
Private Online Digital Classes
Digital learning support for parents to assist in the academic journey of their children. Classes are held every Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday. These pre-recorded classes offer structured learning and are intended for single-user access only.
