Privacy Policy

How E-Vidya Academy collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

📅 Effective Date: 11 March 2026

1 Introduction

Welcome to E-Vidya Academy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit or use our website. By using this website, you consent to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please refrain from using our platform.

2 Information We Collect

To provide our services, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Email Address: For communication and service updates.
  • Age: To ensure compliance with age restrictions and provide appropriate services.
  • Phone Number: For communication and service delivery.
  • Name: For identification and personalisation of services.
  • Address: For record-keeping and location-specific services.

3 How We Use Your Information

  • Service Delivery: To provide educational content and personal development resources.
  • Communication: To send notifications, updates, and information related to our services via email or phone.
  • Personalisation: To tailor content and features based on your preferences and location, and to improve our services.

4 Information Sharing and Disclosure

Third-Party Service Providers: We may share your information with trusted third-party providers who help us operate the website. They are obligated to protect your information and use it only as directed.

Legal Compliance: We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to a legal process such as a court order or subpoena.

Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred to the new owner as part of the transaction.

5 Third-Party Links

The website includes links to third-party websites. These sites are not controlled by us and may have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review their policies before engaging with them. We are not responsible for their practices.

6 Data Security

We employ reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Users are encouraged to use strong passwords and never share their login credentials with anyone.

7 User Consent

By using this website, you consent to the collection, use, and sharing of your personal information as outlined in this Privacy Policy. Continued use after changes to this policy will be deemed acceptance of those changes.

8 User Rights

Access and Correction: You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you and to request corrections if it is inaccurate or incomplete.

Opt-Out: You can opt out of marketing communications at any time by following the unsubscribe instructions in our emails.

Data Deletion: You may request deletion of your personal information. We will comply unless we have a legitimate legal or business reason to retain it.

9 Retention of Information

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes in this Privacy Policy, or as required by law. When no longer needed, we will securely delete or anonymise it.

10 Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential Cookies: To enable basic website functionality and secure access.
  • Performance Cookies: To collect data on how users interact with the site to improve performance.
  • Functionality Cookies: To remember your preferences and settings.
  • Third-Party Cookies: Services like Google Forms may set cookies on your device. Please review their privacy policies for details.
You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect site functionality. By continuing to use our website, you consent to cookies as described above.

11 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Significant changes may be communicated via email or prominent notices on the website.
