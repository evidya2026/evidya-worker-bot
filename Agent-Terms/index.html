You must be at least 18 years of age, capable of entering into a valid contract under the Indian Contract Act, 1872, and must provide true, accurate, and complete information (full name, shop/business name, address, and mobile number) at the time of registration.

2. Nature of the Relationship

  1. You are engaged strictly as an independent, non-exclusive referral agent. Nothing in these Agent Terms creates an employer-employee relationship, partnership, joint venture, or agency of general authority between you and the Proprietor.

3. Your Agent Code

  1. On successful registration, you will receive a unique Agent Code. You are solely responsible for keeping your Agent Code confidential and secure. The Proprietor is not liable for any loss arising from your failure to safeguard your Agent Code.
  2. If your Agent Code is lost, contact the Proprietor directly for assistance; do not attempt to re-register, as this may result in loss of your existing referral/commission history.

4. Your Duties & Responsibilities

  1. You may refer prospective Subscribers by submitting the referred student's name, class, and phone number through the Agent Portal, only with the prior knowledge and consent of that student's parent/guardian.
  2. 5. Commission

  1. The Proprietor shall pay you commission, calculated at the rate and structure communicated to you from time to time (visible in your Agent Portal commission ledger), for each referral that results in a paying Subscriber, subject to applicable tax deduction at source (TDS) under the Income Tax Act, 1961.

  3. You are solely responsible for your own tax filings in respect of commission earned, save for TDS which the Proprietor will deduct and deposit as required by law.

6. Confidentiality

You shall keep confidential, both during and after your engagement as an Agent, your Agent Code, any referral/commission data visible to you, and any business or pricing information of the Proprietor that is not publicly available.

7. Data Protection Obligations

In relation to any personal data of a referred student/parent that you submit, you confirm that: (a) you have obtained that person's (or their guardian's) informed consent to share their name, class, and phone number with the Proprietor for the purpose of the referral; (b) you will not use such data for any other purpose, including your own independent marketing; (c) you will not retain such data beyond what is necessary to complete the referral.
